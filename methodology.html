Editorial standards

Evidence first. Human review always.

Our work is built around source verification, transparent distinctions, and corrections when new evidence warrants them.

Research and verification

We prioritize primary sources such as government reports, legislative text, court records, public datasets, and original investigations. We distinguish verified facts, allegations, interpretation, and opinion.

Data integrity

We identify the source, timeframe, population, geographic scope, and definitions behind every dataset. We distinguish correlation from causation, estimates from observed figures, and preliminary findings from finalized results. We do not combine incompatible measures, present incomplete data as comprehensive, or invent missing statistics. Each research tool documents its own limitations and methodology.

Editorial approval

AI helps identify sources and organize research; publication decisions remain with a human editor. New stories and revisions should be reviewed before release.

Corrections and contact
