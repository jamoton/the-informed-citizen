Sources & Editorial Standards

We distinguish verified facts, allegations, interpretations, and opinions. We prioritize original reporting, court documents, government records, legislative texts, and primary evidence.

We check material counterevidence, preserve attribution, correct errors, and do not publish AI-generated claims without human review.

Dashboard-specific definitions and sources are available in the Immigration Crime Dashboard under Sources & Methodology.

Publication policy: Proposed stories and automated research are drafts until approved by a human editor.
