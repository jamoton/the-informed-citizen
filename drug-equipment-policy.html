Methodology and original sources

The legal dataset distinguishes (1) whether an SSP may operate, (2) whether pipes may be distributed through it, and (3) whether an organization actually distributes pipes. The third question is not answered by statewide law. Program counts are self-reported and may be incomplete. Funding amounts, operators, and city-level policy changes require separate verification.
