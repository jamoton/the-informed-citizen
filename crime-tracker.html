Impaired Driving & Traffic Crime

Explore DUI/DWI offenses and alcohol-impaired driving fatalities. These are generally traffic offenses, not automatically violent crimes; fatal incidents may involve additional charges.

How the sections fit together: A murder in a city is both a violent offense and an urban incident. A DUI is primarily a traffic offense, even when its consequences are severe. Immigration status describes a population, not an offense. These categories overlap, and figures across them should not be added together.
