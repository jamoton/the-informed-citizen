The FBI and CDC measure different events. City boundaries, agency jurisdictions, and county mortality geography are not interchangeable.

Four checks before comparing cities

01
Choose the same offense definition

Homicide, robbery, assault, burglary, and DUI should not be combined indiscriminately.

02
Confirm reporting coverage

Verify complete reporting months and the agency's jurisdiction.

03
Normalize population

Use rates per 100,000 residents when appropriate, alongside counts.

04
Separate correlation from causation

Changes in enforcement, reporting, population, or policy do not alone establish what caused crime to rise or fall.

How to interpret the atlas

A
Verified facts

The national 2024 FBI estimates shown here are sourced. No local offense counts are asserted.

B
What “urban” means

A geographic lens, not a separate FBI crime classification. City police jurisdictions and metropolitan areas are different boundaries.

C
What is excluded

No unsupported city rankings, inferred demographics, or causal claims. DUI is not automatically counted as FBI violent crime.

D
Human review

New city comparisons require source verification, reporting completeness checks, and editorial approval before publication.
